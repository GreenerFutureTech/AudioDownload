AUDIODOWNLOAD / HELP

Keep the audio you create.

Find the original file, assemble a supported stream, or record what plays in a tab.

Find your song

  1. Reload the song's page after installing the extension, then play it.
  2. Open AudioDownload. A source link is an audio URL explicitly included by the site in a stream's src parameter. Try its Download button first. Availability, quality, and length depend on the server.
  3. For an HLS playlist, choose Inspect HLS to look for source links and see its duration. Choose Assemble audio to fetch its initialization file and segments. When finished, go to Saved audio → Download.

A blob: URL attached to MediaSource is a reference to a player stream, not an ordinary audio download. The extension observes the underlying network requests instead.

Start, stop, and download a recording

  1. Play audio and open the extension on that tab.
  2. Choose Start recording. The timer and red dot show that capture is running. The extension records tab audio, not your microphone.
  3. Choose Stop & Save. This control is always at the top of the popup. You can close and reopen the popup or switch tabs and still stop the active recording.
  4. Open Saved audio and choose Download. Tab recordings use WebM/Opus, not MP3.

After Stop & Save, that page's recordings appear in the same list with Download WEBM buttons, and are also available in Saved audio. M4A source links are additional download options and do not replace recordings.

Capture stops and saves at 60 minutes or approximately 128 MB, or when the captured tab closes. Previously persisted chunks can be recovered after a browser restart. The final unsaved chunk may be lost if the browser exits unexpectedly.

Supported streams

Finite, unencrypted HLS with one audio-only fragmented MP4 initialization file and a continuous sequence of fragments; packed AAC or MP3 segments. Master playlists select the default standalone audio rendition, or the highest bandwidth audio-only variant. Assembly preserves the audio served by the site and does not transcode it.

Live/incomplete playlists, encryption, discontinuities, missing segments, MPEG-TS, video renditions, and DASH assembly are not supported. DASH entries can be saved as manifest text. The extension does not decrypt protected playback.

If a download fails

Play the song again to obtain fresh links. Sites may require a login, cookies, authorization headers, or a particular request origin; extension downloads cannot reproduce every site's player session. Browser settings pages cannot be scanned or recorded. Brave Shields and extension site access can affect detection. In the extension's details, allow access to the site you want to inspect.

Local by design

There is no account, analytics, or upload server. Detected URLs are kept in browser session storage and cleared for a tab when it navigates or closes. Recordings and assembled audio are kept in the extension's local IndexedDB storage until deleted or the extension is removed. Browser storage limits may apply; download copies of audio you want to keep.

The extension requests HTTP/HTTPS site access to observe media response headers and find audio elements. Downloads permission saves files; offscreen and tab capture permissions allow recording after the popup closes. Recording starts only when you click Start recording.
